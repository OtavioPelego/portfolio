Getting found · search on Google and in AI

Your customer asks Google. And, more and more, asks AI.

Showing up in search used to be enough. Today a lot of people ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot "who does this in my city?" — and the answer comes from sites the AI can read, understand and cite. I get your business ready for both worlds: classic search and AI search.

What I set up

Two fronts that now go together: showing up for Google (SEO) and being understood by AI (GEO).

  • Google Business Profile — creating or recovering it, verification, the right categories, hours, photos and service area.
  • Showing up in local search — complete, consistent information, which is what Google uses to decide who to show.
  • Readable by AI — structured data and an llms.txt file that introduces your company in machine language, so the assistant understands what you do and who for.
  • Content the AI can cite — clear answers to the questions your customer actually asks, in the shape an assistant extracts and recommends.
  • Reviews — a simple way to ask customers for one, and a routine for answering them all.
  • Professional e-mail and Drive — contact@yourcompany.com that doesn't land in spam, and files organised with the right access for each person.
