My story

I started in the field, not at a screen. I'm an agronomist with a master's and a PhD — seven years learning how to ask a dataset a question, and to distrust an answer that looks too good. That's where the part of me that won't ship a chart without checking where the number came from was formed.

Then came technology, and the world. I had an innovation stint in Silicon Valley and spent almost two years at Machines Like Me GmbH in Germany, an AI and automation company serving the European market — working on project management, the corporate website and the optimisation of internal automations. That's where I learned that good technology disappears: it gives time back to people, and nobody has to think about it.

Today I bring both together at Phosphorus, my company. I'm a certified Scrum Master (PSM I) and Product Owner, with more than seven years in the tech ecosystem. I work as what I like to call a Centurion: the technical person who doesn't stay in the slide deck — who goes down into the operation, understands what's really blocking it, and builds the solution alongside you.

If your problem involves data, process, or the distance between the two, it's probably one for me.
