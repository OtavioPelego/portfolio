Automation + AI

Your team is doing a robot's job.

Copy from one system, paste into another, double-check, resend, remember to follow up. That isn't human work — it's a task waiting for someone to automate it. I connect what you already use and let the information move on its own.

Where it usually hurts

The same data, typed three timesIt arrives on WhatsApp, gets copied to a spreadsheet, then re-entered into the system. Every hop is a chance to get it wrong.
The thing that depends on someone rememberingFollowing up a quote, chasing a document, flagging a due date. When the day gets busy, it slips.
Expensive people on cheap tasksYou hired someone to think, and they spend half the day organising files.

What I do

  • System integration — your CRM, spreadsheet, e-mail, WhatsApp and ERP talking to each other over APIs, with no typing in between.
  • Automatic flows — the process fires by itself when the condition happens: a lead arrives, a deadline hits, a payment lands.
  • AI agents — for the work that needs interpreting, not just moving: reading a document, qualifying a lead, summarising a conversation, classifying an order.
  • Robots for systems with no API — when the software is closed, I automate the interface (RPA) so the project doesn't stall.
  • An alarm when it breaks — serious automation tells you when it fails. You shouldn't find out from a customer.
  • Everything documented — you know what runs, where and why. No black box.
