Visual Data Analytics

Your data exists. Clarity is what's missing.

Every company piles up data. Almost none can look at it and actually decide something. I build the bridge: I take what's trapped in spreadsheets, systems and PDFs and hand it back as something your team understands without training.

What I deliver

It isn't "a dashboard". It's the whole path, from the raw data to the screen someone actually uses.

  • Live dashboards — your operation in real time, refreshing on its own, open in a browser or on a phone.
  • Maps and 3D visuals — when the data is geographic, seeing the territory changes what you notice.
  • Reports you explore — instead of a dead PDF, a page where the reader clicks and answers their own question.
  • Presentations with a wow factor — the same study you'd hand over as a slide deck, turned into a page that impresses your client.
  • The pipeline behind it — extracting, cleaning and joining the sources, so the number arrives correct and arrives by itself.
  • Your autonomy — I hand it over working and explain how to run it. I don't build dependency on me.

Charqueada São João · tourism, tours and events

From a year of spreadsheets to a strategic radar

A dense year of history was being used for lookups and nothing else. I built a Business Intelligence ecosystem in Looker Studio focused on relationships and commercial opportunity: extraction and structuring of a base of nearly 10,000 leads, plus an influence map in a Sankey diagram tracing "who brings whom".

Leadership moved from static spreadsheets to visual control over where visitors come from, who influences them, and how the season swings.
