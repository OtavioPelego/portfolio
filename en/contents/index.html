Content & Attention with AI

You know you need to publish. Time is what you don't have.

Your company isn't short of things to say — it's short of a production line. I build the machine that turns what you already know into content you can publish consistently: text, image, audio and video, in your brand's voice.

Where it usually stalls

What I build for you

  • Text — posts, articles, newsletters and scripts, written in the vocabulary of your niche, not the robot's.
  • Image — covers, carousels and visual pieces that respect your brand identity.
  • Audio — podcast episodes generated from a topic, with voices that sound like real hosts.
  • Video — reels and short videos from the script, with the visuals assembled and synced.
  • Active research — the machine sweeps the web for what actually matters in your niche before writing a word.
  • You stay in control — nothing is published without your approval, and the tone adjusts as you correct it.

An honest caveat

What's a service and what's still a lab

Producing text, image, audio and video with AI is something I do today, as a service, with a human in the loop. The fully autonomous part — agents publishing to your calendar with nobody in the middle — is what I'm still building. I'd rather tell you that now than sell you a roadmap as if it were a product.
