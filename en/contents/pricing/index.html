Three rules that prevent misunderstandings

The script is on you The list price assumes you bring an approved script or storyboard. If I create the concept from scratch, a conception fee applies — agreed up front, no surprises.
Two rounds of revisions Every package includes up to 2 rounds of editing revisions. That's enough to get pacing, cuts and details right.
Regenerating a scene is extra If you want to change a scene's concept after it's finished, it has to be generated again: $20 per scene. Changing the idea is different from adjusting the cut.
