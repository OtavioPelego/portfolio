The projects

Working MVP · seeking clinical partners

O2C Nutri — from a macro assistant to an Open Health ecosystem

Adherence to nutrition protocols breaks down through friction: apps demand that the patient type everything, and they quit within weeks. Meanwhile the professional follows up from memory, with no real data between one appointment and the next.

The MVP already does the essentials: sign-up, goal setting, a strategy built from biometrics, macro calculation, and an AI engine that generates custom recipes — including reading a photo of the fridge, to remove manual entry altogether.

The bigger vision is an Open Health ecosystem: a platform where the user records their life history — habits, lab results, water, training — and health professionals supervise and iterate on that data in an integrated way.

What I'm looking for Nutritionists, doctors and mentors with clinical expertise and an audience, to iterate on this foundation and turn it into a business. The B2B2C channel is the thesis: the specialist brings their patient base, and the cost of acquisition dilutes.

Working MVP · seeking commercial application

An AI-run podcast studio

An engine that creates whole episodes from a single topic: it sweeps the web in real time for the most relevant sources in the niche, generates a natural dialogue tuned to the brand's tone, and synthesises voices that debate the content like real hosts — audio ready to publish.

It works today. What doesn't exist yet is the product around it: an interface for the end client, plans, automatic distribution.

What I'm looking for A commercial partner or a media company that wants to take this to market — or pilot clients willing to use it in production while we mature the product.
